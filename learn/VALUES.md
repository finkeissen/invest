# Values

This document declares the **external values** that motivated the framework.

Values are not derived from the framework.
They are not justified by it.
They are inputs, not outputs.

---

## Declared values

The framework was developed under the working assumption that learning
should be **transparent** (a learner can see their own state),
**falsifiable** (claims about understanding can be tested), and
**non-coercive** (a learner is not optimized against their own interests).

These are commitments, not theorems.
They can be revised; the framework would then have to be revised with them.

---

## What this means structurally

- Transparency motivates the explicit Learner State and the auditability
  of every transition.
- Falsifiability motivates the dimension-shift probe and the treatment
  of every gap as a testable claim.
- Non-coercion motivates STOP as a first-class outcome, and the refusal
  to substitute system-internal goals for learner-declared goals.

These motivations explain *why* the structure looks the way it does.
They do not validate it.

---

## What this is not

- Not a manifesto
- Not a worldview
- Not a moral framework
- Not a curriculum policy

A broader exploration of related normative questions is maintained
separately and is not part of this repository.
