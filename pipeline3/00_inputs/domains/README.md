# Domains

This folder defines the major investment domains used across the repository.

Examples include:
- health
- education
- finance
- relationships
- trust
- institutional quality
- meaning and spiritual orientation
- strategic optionality

The goal is to keep domain language stable across hypotheses, portfolios, and reports.


## Protective investment extension

Domains should also be readable as protection domains.

For each domain, ask:
- what condition of a good life or functioning it stabilizes,
- what threshold failure looks like,
- what indicators warn of degradation,
- and which investments serve prevention, mitigation, buffering, transfer, recovery, or adaptation.
