# Actors

This folder defines the entities that matter strategically inside the modeled world.

Use this folder for actor taxonomies and actor definitions across multiple scales.

## Main actor levels

- persons
- households and families
- communities and groups
- institutions and firms
- states and alliances
- non-state disruptive actors

## Each actor description should clarify

- role
- incentives
- constraints
- resources
- time horizon
- relevance to later agent modeling

## Design note

A group is not just a large person.

A person, a household, a company, a church, and a state all invest differently. They face different constraints, different forms of gain, and different kinds of failure.


## Protective investment extension

Actor definitions should also clarify:
- which conditions of life or function the actor must defend,
- which threshold failures would be catastrophic for that actor,
- what forms of buffering, transfer, recovery, or adaptation are realistically available,
- and where the actor may mistake formal protection for real recoverability.

This matters because persons, households, firms, communities, and states do not merely pursue gain. They also try to preserve viability under stress.
